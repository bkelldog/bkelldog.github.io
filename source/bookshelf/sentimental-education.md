---
title: Sentimental Education
author: Gustave Flaubert
published: 1869
pages: 460
---

Another observational French novel, the likes of which include [Là-Bas](/bookshelf#là-bas) and [Bel-Ami](/bookshelf#bel-ami), this time regarding a young romantic named Frédéric who yearns after a married woman Madame Arnoux. It gives a glimpse into Parisian life at the time of the February Revolution of 1848, and in the fashion of these French novels also crystallizes Parisian social life and all its idiosyncracies. Drawn out at times but utterly pleasant to read.
