---
title: Les Fleurs du mal
author: Charles Baudelaire
published: 1857
pages: 312
---

The content appears on the surface to be a firm repudiation of the values of upright civilization: and at its root that's precisely what it is, a rejection of the vanity of bourgeois society and all its vapid customs. The presentation of a Satanism is largely a scare tactic meant to drive away intellectual weaklings and those who cling to shallow ideas of Christianity. Yet these poems are some of the most beautiful outpourings of the heart that I've encountered. I read the French version from an edition I bought in Bologna, and translate it as I go.
