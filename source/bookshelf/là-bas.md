title: Là-Bas
author: J.K. Huysmans
published: 1891
pages: 287
read: 2025-10-24
---

A jarring and refreshing French novel whose main theme is Satanism, but only observationally. The book runs on parallel stories of the 15th century noble and "alleged" Satanist, torturer, and child-killer Gilles de Rais, alongside his modern biographer Durtal, who discovers similar Satanism in Belle Époque Paris. By seducing the wife of an acquaintance, Durtal gains admission to a Black Mass organized by France's leading Satanist priest, Father Docre. Despite such vivid subject matter, not much action takes place in the book, which is to its credit as an observation on human nature; rather the ideas presented show off the force of the novel.
