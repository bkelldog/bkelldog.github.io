Benvenuto Cellini's account of his own life and career as a titan of goldsmithery and sculpture during the Renaissance. He narrates many adventures through Italy and France in a time when people were still free, including his imprisonment in Castel Sant'Angelo, various murders, countless arguments, and his crafting his famous works, especially the legendary [Perseus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perseus_with_the_Head_of_Medusa).
