---
title: Bel-Ami
author: Guy de Maupassant
published: 1885
pages: 108
read: 2025-09-13
---

Yet another observational French novel commenting on upper middle class social life; this time following a 'scoundrel' named Duroy as he climbs the social ladder by seducing and manipulating a series of women in his circle. This book is complete fun and is powered by Duroy's apathy, or perhaps lack of self-restraint, in handling these women: he seems to feel either nothing, or feel true passions which are so fleeting that as soon as a woman falls for him, he set a higher target. The theme is again how one might poke holes in bourgeois status anxiety and turn people's 'social striver' behavior against them.
