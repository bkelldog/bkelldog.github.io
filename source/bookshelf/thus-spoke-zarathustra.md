It's quite clear to me from Nietzsche's account of writing *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* that he was in fact overtaken by an [*ergreifer*](https://ia802807.us.archive.org/3/items/carl-jung-on-wotan/Carl%20Jung%20-%20On%20Wotan.pdf) which drew this work out of him. I consider it inspired literature, so unlike anything else of its time but yet so characteristic of Nietzsche's works. It is for me the encapsulation of the transcendent spirit which Nietzsche sought and saw in the Greeks. And to me it also carries a scent of the Gospels: many passages I could imagine Christ speaking rather than an imagined Zarathustra, and this is how I know that it converges on Truth.
